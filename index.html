Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Campaign Studio collects the following information:

  • Email addresses and contact information you import
  • Campaign data and email content you create
  • LinkedIn profile information (when you connect via OAuth)
  • Email tracking data (opens, clicks) for campaigns you send

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send email campaigns on your behalf
  • Track campaign performance (opens, clicks)
  • Provide LinkedIn OAuth authentication
  • Improve our services

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your server in a secure database. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. LinkedIn OAuth

When you connect LinkedIn, we request access to your basic profile information (name, email) for authentication purposes only. We do not post to your LinkedIn account or access your connections.

5. Email Security

Email campaigns are sent through secure SMTP connections. Your SMTP credentials are encrypted and stored securely.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Disconnect LinkedIn integration
  • Export your campaign data
